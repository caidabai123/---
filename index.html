关于领智优选

About Top Wise Choice

领智优选（Top Wise Choice）是一家深耕全球青年跨境服务的国际化企业，总部立足中国广州，海外布局覆盖澳大利亚悉尼、新西兰奥克兰、英国伦敦三大核心海外城市，搭建起辐射欧亚、大洋洲的一站式跨境服务网络。

公司聚焦三大核心业务：海外全职就业安置、海外白领精品实习、全球国际交流项目。针对全球热门的美国、澳大利亚、加拿大、英国、爱尔兰、新西兰、新加坡等国家，为客户提供精准的短期工作签证、打工度假签证、雇主担保就业、白领职场实习、国际文化交流等全链条服务，业务涵盖酒店餐饮服务、护理康养、高级技能蓝领等主流海外就业岗位，以及商科、IT、金融、市场营销等高端白领实习项目，同时配套多元的国际研学、义工交流、青年成长项目。

自成立以来，我们始终坚守品牌初心：以世界为舞台，以交流为纽带，陪伴逐梦青年前行，让每一份坚持都收获想要的生活。

Top Wise Choice is an international enterprise focusing on global youth cross-border services. Headquartered in Guangzhou, China, we have overseas branches in Sydney (Australia), Auckland (New Zealand), and London (UK), building a one-stop global service network covering Europe, Oceania and North America.

We specialize in three core businesses: Overseas Employment, Overseas White-Collar Internships, and Global Exchange Programs. Covering mainstream countries including the United States, Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, New Zealand and Singapore, we provide full-cycle services including short-term work visas, working holiday visas, employer-sponsored employment, professional internships and international cultural exchanges.

Our brand mission has always been: Take the world as the stage, communication as the link, accompany young dreamers forward, and let every perseverance harvest the life they desire.
